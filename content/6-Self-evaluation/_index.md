During my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Co., Ltd.** from **22/6/2026** to **15/8/2026**, I had the opportunity to learn, develop, and apply the knowledge I had acquired at school to a real working environment.

Throughout the internship, I participated in studying and implementing a workshop on **High Availability on AWS**, focusing on designing and deploying an application system that can tolerate failures and maintain stable operations.

Through this process, I improved my skills in AWS cloud computing, system deployment and operations, architecture analysis, troubleshooting, system testing, technical writing, and reporting. In addition, the internship helped me strengthen self-learning ability, work planning, and the capability to apply theoretical knowledge to real-world problems.

Besides technical knowledge, the internship also helped me develop a professional working attitude, discipline, responsibility, initiative, and time management skills. These are important experiences that better prepare me for the professional work environment in the future.

### Areas for improvement

* During the internship, I realized that I still need to improve in a few areas. First, I need to be more proactive in studying and solving problems, especially when dealing with new topics or situations that I have limited practical experience with. In addition, I need to improve my time management and work organization skills so I can complete tasks on schedule and more efficiently.
* In terms of work attitude, I need to continue improving my initiative, confidence in communication, and ability to collaborate with team members. I also need to maintain the habit of recording progress, updating work status, and reporting tasks regularly to make the work process clearer and more professional.
* Based on the points above, I identify these as skills that need continued improvement in the future to increase my work efficiency and adapt better to a professional working environment.
